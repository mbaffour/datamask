Choose your protection mode
Three tiers — from simple obfuscation to cryptographically irreversible anonymization.
🔵
Lite
Adds noise, shuffles, or scales values. Structure and scale are loosely preserved. Fast and suitable for internal testing.
reversible with effort
🟡
Strong
Normalizes to [0,1], applies non-linear sigmoid distortion with random parameters, then cross-mixes columns. Hard to reverse without a reference dataset — but the transform is monotonic, so rank order is largely preserved.
hard to reverse · preserves rank
🟣
Vault
Uses SHA-256 cryptographic hashing to seed a PRNG per cell. Generates statistically plausible synthetic values with zero mathematical link to originals.
cryptographically irreversible
